# Three-minute demonstration

**0:00–0:20 — Problem.** Colleges store attendance, marks and placement assessments separately. PULSEIQ joins institutional records, explains support needs and helps faculty agree on actions. Students do not have to upload their entire history.

**0:20–0:50 — Data quality.** Show the eight bundled CSV sources, the reporting period, missing-data warnings and source coverage. State that the full college demo is synthetic.

**0:50–1:30 — Cohort view.** Show separate academic and placement support counts. Filter Strong academics with placement gap. Explain that segments overlap and scores are support indicators, not outcome probabilities.

**1:30–2:15 — Evidence.** Open DEMO001. A80/T90/L70/P40/S60/E50 yields 68.0. Its academics have no observed rule trigger but its placement mean and coding assessment trigger support. Show field values and source dates.

**2:15–2:45 — Action.** Choose coding practice, assign an owner alias and create a review date. On Action Board, show status updates, the review note and revision history export.

**2:45–3:00 — Scope.** State that this release uses rules, session state and human review. A real UCI academic derivative is included separately; its missing categories remain unknown. Authenticated institutional roles, persistent storage, approved connectors and independently evaluated prediction are future work.

If time allows after the main demo, show What-if Simulator and its explicit non-prediction notice. Increase all three placement assessments to 80 for DEMO001: the scenario score becomes 78.0 and the placement trigger clears. The baseline remains 68.0.
